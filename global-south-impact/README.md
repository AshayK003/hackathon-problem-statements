# 🌍 Global South Impact

**10 AI/ML projects** solving invisible infrastructure problems in the developing world.

---

## Why This Track

Commercial SaaS cannot reach customers who can't pay market rates. But a **payment bypass exists** — governments, NGOs, climate funds, and corporate sustainability programs can fund infrastructure that serves underserved populations.

Each of these problems involves **AI coordinating fragmented, non-digitized actors** across systems where first-world solutions never compete.
